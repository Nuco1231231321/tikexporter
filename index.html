TikExporter Open the viewer

Watch public TikTok without an account, and export comments to CSV

TikExporter is a browser-based viewer for public TikTok content. Paste a profile or video link, read the thread, and download the comments as a spreadsheet. No app, no TikTok login, and no profile view from this viewer.

Open TikExporter

What you can open

  • Public profiles and their video lists
  • Individual videos, including shared links
  • Stories and reposts that TikTok shows to visitors
  • Public LIVEs
  • Comment threads on public videos, with replies

Everything renders in the browser. There is no desktop program, no extension, and no account to create before you can look at a public page.

How to export comments to CSV

  1. Copy a public video link, or type the creator's username.
  2. Open the video and load its comment thread.
  3. Choose the number of comments you need and start the export.
  4. The browser downloads a .csv file that opens in Excel, Google Sheets, Numbers or a database import.

If you only want to read a thread, you do not need to export anything. Viewing is free and the export step is optional.

What the CSV contains

One row per comment, with a header row and UTF-8 encoding:

comment_id,parent_comment_id,is_reply,video_id,username,nickname,user_id,text,likes,reply_count,created_at

Replies keep the id of the comment they answer, so a thread can be rebuilt in a spreadsheet. A timestamp is included for time-based analysis.

Free and paid

Viewing is free. Comment export includes a free allowance: 50 rows per video and 200 rows per month. Larger batches use prepaid credits, and a subscription is optional rather than required.

There is a small sample file if you want to see the format before exporting: sample-comments.csv.

Limits, stated plainly

  • Public content only. Private accounts, friends-only videos and deleted posts cannot be opened.
  • No TikTok login, no posting, no liking, no direct messages.
  • Comments that TikTok does not show to logged-out visitors are not included.
  • TikTok changes its pages regularly, so a page or an export can break until it is fixed.
  • TikExporter is independent and not affiliated with TikTok or ByteDance.

Questions

Do I need a TikTok account?
No. The viewer reads public pages that TikTok serves to any visitor. You never enter TikTok credentials.
Can I export comments from a private video?
No. If TikTok does not show the video or its comments to a logged-out visitor, TikExporter cannot read them.
How many comments can I export for free?
50 rows per video and 200 rows per month. Larger batches use prepaid credits.
Does the creator see that I watched?
The viewer does not sign in to TikTok and does not report a profile view. The page request itself is a normal request for public content.
Why did an export fail?
Usually because TikTok changed the page or rate-limited the request. Reload the video and try again; persistent failures are worth reporting.
Where do the comments go?
Into a CSV file on your device. Saved lists stay in your browser's local storage on the main site.
